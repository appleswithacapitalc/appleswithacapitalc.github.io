Sunrises

Around October 2026, I thought about how many sunrises each of us is privy to in our lifetime. By my estimate, it's about thirty thousand (30,000) sunrises, which corresponds to an age of 82 years and 50 days, give or take. I was sad to realize this, because it had seemed like somehow sunrises were an infinite resource, even though logic dictates otherwise. So I am challenging myself to see three thousand (3,000) sunrises (and possibly sunsets) by the time I die, and this blog/website serves as a record of my progress. I estimate I'll achieve this goal around 2040 😊. Writing each post is still a work in progress and admittedly, I'm using the term sunrises loosely as some of these images might not necessarily be taken at dawn, but I will still count them.
