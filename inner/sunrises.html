Sunrises

Around October 2026, I thought about how many sunrises each of us is privy to in our lifetime. By my estimate, it's about thirty thousand (30,000) sunrises, which corresponds to an age of 82 years and 50 days, give or take. I was sad to realize this, because it somehow seemed like sunrises were an infinite resource, even though logic dictates otherwise. So I am challenging myself to see three thousand (3,000) sunrises (and possibly sunsets) by the time I die, and this blog/website serves as a record of my progress. I estimate I'll achieve this goal around 2040 😊.
