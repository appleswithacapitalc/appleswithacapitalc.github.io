Sunrises

Around October 2026, I thought about how many sunrises each of us is privy to in our lifetime. By my estimate, I settled on thirty thousand (30 000) sunrises, which corresponds to an age of 82 years and 50 days, give or not. I was sad to realize this because it somehow seemed like seeing sunrises was an infinite resource, even though logic dictates otherwise. So I resolved myself to try and see three thousand (3 000) sunrises (and possibly sunsets) by the time I die, and this blog/website serves as a catalogue/record of my resolution.
